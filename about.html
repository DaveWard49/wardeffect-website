Background

The Inventor

David Ward is the sole inventor of Ward Effect Technology, based in Tacloban, Eastern Visayas, Philippines. With over 50 years of research experience and a Six Sigma Black Belt background, Dave has applied rigorous process thinking to the development of sensing technology that breaks fundamental assumptions of the field.

Ward Effect Technology was founded in 2008 to commercialise the core patent portfolio. The humanitarian mission — the water purification bucket — has been the driving goal throughout. All commercial revenue funds the research and development required to validate and open-source this technology.

Dave works alone with electronic components sourced locally in the Philippines and internationally. The Ward Effect Node PCB is designed in KiCad. WardScope software is developed in Python and Flutter. The entire technology stack — hardware, software, and IP — is the work of one person.
