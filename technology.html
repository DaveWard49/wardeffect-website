The Science

How Ward Effect Works

The Ward Effect is not an incremental improvement to existing sensing technology. It is a fundamentally different approach — one that turns a circuit flaw into a precision instrument.

The Core Principle

Deliberate Parasitic Oscillation

Conventional electronics engineering treats parasitic oscillation as a defect to be eliminated. The Ward Effect does the opposite — it deliberately engineers stable parasitic oscillation in an LM386 amplifier circuit, then places the medium being measured physically inside the feedback loop.

When the medium changes — its density, chemical composition, temperature, flow rate, or structural integrity — the oscillation fingerprint changes in measurable, repeatable ways. The circuit becomes a precision sensor without any additional sensing components.

The power supply voltage is a controlled sensing variable, not noise to be filtered. CD4066BE analog switches select EQ settings that shift the oscillation characteristics. The state space is 20 x 100 to the power of 20 unique states per EQ setting — effectively unlimited resolution.

Key Principles

What Makes It Different

Medium Inside the Loop

The medium being measured is not a target — it is a component of the circuit itself. Changes in the medium directly alter circuit behaviour with no intermediary transducer.

Self-Calibrating

The oscillation baseline is established by the medium itself. No external calibration standard is required. The system recalibrates continuously as conditions change.

Voltage as Sensor

Supply voltage is a controlled sensing variable. Varying voltage across the circuit produces different oscillation signatures, multiplying the state space available for measurement.

No Consumables

Unlike chemical testing, Ward Effect requires no reagents, no test strips, no calibration solutions. The sensing element is the circuit itself — indefinite operational life.

Library Configurable

The LEF sparse library system allows the same hardware to measure different properties by loading different measurement libraries. One device, unlimited applications.

Nano-Resolution

The state space available to Ward Effect sensing is effectively unlimited. Detection of parts-per-billion concentrations is achievable with standard consumer-grade electronic components.
