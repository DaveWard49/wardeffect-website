🔓 Open Source Applications (Zero Cost, Zero Royalties)

What Qualifies as Open Source?

  • Drinking Water Purification - Any application where primary purpose is making water safe for human consumption (any scale)
  • Municipal Water Infrastructure - Public water system safety, leak detection, quality monitoring, and purification
  • Critical Public Health - Hospital superbug detection, medical facility pathogen monitoring
  • Infant Safety - Baby formula production facilities, infant food processing contamination monitoring
  • Agricultural Water Supplies - Water for irrigation, livestock, aquaculture for food production (includes devices to remove biofilm from agricultural water pipes)
  • Emergency Relief - Disaster response, refugee camps, humanitarian crises
  • Anything Associated with Drinking Water - If it relates to drinking water safety, access, or quality, it's open source
Scale doesn't matter: Whether you're building one village system or manufacturing 100,000 units for global distribution, water purification is ALWAYS open source.

🤝 Why No Exclusive Licenses?

Even for commercial applications, Ward Effect Technology will NEVER grant exclusive licenses.

Our Reasoning:

  • Monopolies Restrict Innovation - Exclusive licenses create single points of failure
  • Competition Drives Quality - Multiple manufacturers compete on service and price, not licensing lock-in
  • Faster Global Deployment - Any qualified manufacturer can implement immediately
  • Prevents Gatekeeping - No corporation can block humanitarian use by controlling commercial rights
Our Promise: Any manufacturer, anywhere in the world, can license commercial applications on identical terms. We compete on technology quality, not artificial scarcity.
