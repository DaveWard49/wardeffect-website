🔧 Gallery Setup Required

This gallery connects to a Google Photos album. To activate it:

  1. Open Google Photos and create a shared album called "How Your Money Is Spent"
  2. Add your photos and videos with captions
  3. Get the album's share link
  4. Visit PublicAlbum.org to generate embed code
  5. Replace this message with the embed code

After setup, add photos from your phone or computer — the gallery updates automatically.
