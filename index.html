The Science

A Breakthrough 50 Years in the Making

The Ward Effect® deliberately engineers stable parasitic oscillation in a sensing circuit where the medium being measured is physically inside the feedback loop. The oscillation fingerprint changes measurably based on the condition of that medium.

The result: nano-resolution measurement of physical and chemical properties without expensive laboratory equipment, without consumables, without trained operators.

Patents granted in New Zealand (NZ 739314, NZ 770993, NZ 778527), USA, and India. Further patent application currently under examination at IPONZ.
